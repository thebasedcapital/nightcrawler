# Mission: Systematic Review — [Research Question]

**Type:** research
**Created:** [date]

## Research Question

[Specific, answerable question: "Does X improve Y compared to Z?"]

## Objective

Conduct a structured systematic review following PRISMA-like methodology. Collect evidence, assess quality, and synthesize conclusions.

## Inclusion Criteria

- Published 2022-2026
- Peer-reviewed or preprint with code/data
- Directly addresses the research question
- Reports quantitative results

## Exclusion Criteria

- Blog posts or opinion pieces (unless they contain original data)
- Papers without reproducible methodology
- Duplicate studies from same group

## Depth Targets

- [ ] Search phase: query arXiv, Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar for relevant papers
- [ ] Screen phase: review titles/abstracts, apply inclusion/exclusion criteria
- [ ] Extract phase: for each included paper, extract key metrics and findings
- [ ] Quality assessment: rate each paper's methodology (strong/moderate/weak)
- [ ] Synthesis: aggregate findings, identify consensus and disagreements
- [ ] Conclusions: answer the research question with confidence level
- [ ] Limitations: what this review cannot answer and why

## Source Requirements

- Search at least 3 databases/sources
- Minimum 8 papers included after screening
- Document exclusion reasons for rejected papers

## Output Artifacts

- `research/review-[topic].md` — Full systematic review
- `research/review-[topic]-evidence-table.md` — Extracted data table
- `research/review-[topic]-sources.md` — Bibliography with inclusion/exclusion notes

## Success Criteria

- [ ] All depth targets complete
- [ ] Evidence table populated
- [ ] Research question answered with confidence level
- [ ] Methodology transparent and reproducible
