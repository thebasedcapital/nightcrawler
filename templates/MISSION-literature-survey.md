# Mission: Literature Survey — [Topic]

**Type:** research
**Created:** [date]

## Objective

Conduct a comprehensive survey of the current state of [topic]. Map the landscape of approaches, key players, and open problems.

## Depth Targets

- [ ] Identify the top 10 most-cited papers in this field (last 3 years)
- [ ] Categorize approaches into 3-5 major paradigms/schools of thought
- [ ] For each paradigm: key papers, main claims, strengths, weaknesses
- [ ] Identify 5+ active research groups and their focus areas
- [ ] Map the evolution: what changed from 2023 → 2024 → 2025 → 2026?
- [ ] Identify the 3 most promising directions (with justification)
- [ ] List open problems and gaps in the literature
- [ ] Synthesize into a coherent narrative

## Source Requirements

- Minimum 15 unique sources
- At least 5 peer-reviewed papers
- At least 3 from 2025-2026
- Include both academic and industry perspectives

## Output Artifacts

- `research/survey-[topic].md` — Main survey document
- `research/survey-[topic]-taxonomy.md` — Categorization of approaches
- `research/survey-[topic]-sources.md` — Annotated bibliography

## Success Criteria

- [ ] All depth targets complete
- [ ] 15+ unique real sources cited
- [ ] Clear taxonomy of approaches
- [ ] Open problems identified
- [ ] Narrative coherent and well-structured
