## Objective

Deeply analyze a specific paper, understand its contributions, evaluate its claims, and assess its implications for our work.

## Target Paper

- **Title:** [Full paper title]
- **Authors:** [Author list]
- **URL:** [arXiv/DOI link]

## Depth Targets

- [ ] Read and understand the full paper (abstract, intro, methods, results, discussion)
- [ ] Summarize the paper's core contribution in 2-3 paragraphs
- [ ] Evaluate the methodology — is it sound? What are the limitations?
- [ ] Find and read the paper's key references (at least 5)
- [ ] Find papers that cite this paper (forward citations) — what was the impact?
- [ ] Identify what's novel vs what's incremental
- [ ] Assess reproducibility — is the code available? Are the experiments described clearly?
- [ ] Write implications for our work

## Source Requirements

- The target paper itself
- At least 5 of its references (read, not just cited)
- At least 3 forward citations
- Any related blog posts or discussions
- Flag any claim with only 1 source as [UNVERIFIED]

## Output Artifacts

- `research/deepdive-[short-name]-analysis.md` — Full analysis
- `research/deepdive-[short-name]-sources.md` — Bibliography

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Paper fully read and understood
- [ ] Methodology evaluated
- [ ] Key references read
- [ ] Forward citations checked
- [ ] Implications written
- [ ] Analysis is honest about limitations
