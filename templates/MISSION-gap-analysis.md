**Type:** research

## Objective

Identify what's missing in [field/topic]. Find the unasked questions, the unexplored combinations, and the assumptions nobody is challenging.

## Depth Targets

- [ ] Review current state of the field (use existing research if available)
- [ ] Identify explicit gaps authors mention ("future work" sections)
- [ ] Identify implicit gaps (what assumptions are shared across all papers?)
- [ ] Find fields that SHOULD connect but DON'T cite each other

- [ ] For each direction: feasibility assessment, estimated impact, required resources
- [ ] Rank directions by impact-to-effort ratio

## Source Requirements

- Minimum 10 unique sources
- At least 3 "future work" sections from major papers
- At least 2 sources from adjacent fields (not the primary field)

## Output Artifacts

- `research/gaps-[topic].md` — Gap analysis with ranked directions
- `research/gaps-[topic]-sources.md` — Bibliography

## Success Criteria

- [ ] All depth targets complete
- [ ] 3+ novel directions proposed
